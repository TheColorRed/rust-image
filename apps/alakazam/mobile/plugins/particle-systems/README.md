Add moving sparkles, snow, smoke and more to your photos with the Particle Maker workspace.

## What you can do

- Open the Particle Maker from the plugin's workspace.
- Make your own particle systems: choose where particles are born, how big, how many, what color, how they move and fade, whether they leave a trail, and whether they make more particles of their own.
- Watch your system play live as you change it, or stop it at any moment of its life. Change the backdrop, or turn it off to see the particles on their own.
- Start from Fire, Snow, Sparks or Smoke, or from a blank system.
- Every system you make is saved automatically, so you can use it again on other photos.

## Getting started

1. Install the plugin from the Plugins tab.
2. Open **Particle Maker**.
3. Pick a starting point, or choose a saved system, and change what you like. The preview shows it over a plain backdrop, because a system is not made for one photo.

```
Plugins -> Particle Systems -> Particle Maker
```

## Notes

Systems are kept on your device and shared by all your projects. They stay when you remove the plugin and are found again if you install it again. Putting a saved system on a photo is not built yet.
