# Object Remover

**ID:** `alakazam.object-remover`

Adds **Remove Objects** to the **Cleanup** tab. Circle something you don't want, such as a stranger or a sign, then tap **Remove** and the area is filled in to match its surroundings.

## Fast or best quality

Remove Objects offers two choices above the **Remove** button:

- **Object removal (fast):** quick, and a good first choice for small things like a sign, a stray person or a spot.
- **Object removal (best quality):** takes longer, but fills big areas and repeating patterns like bricks, fences, tiles or grass more cleanly.

Tap the one you want. The first one in the list is used until you pick another, and Alakazam remembers your pick for the tool. If the fast one leaves smudges or breaks a pattern, undo and try again with best quality.

Both are downloaded when you install Object Remover. They show up in the **AI models** section of **Plugins**, where you can delete one to free space. Once downloaded, they work offline.

## How to use it

### Open Remove Objects

Open a photo and choose the **Cleanup** tab, then **Remove Objects**. If it isn't there, install Object Remover from **Plugins**.

### Circle the object

Remove Objects opens ready to draw. With your finger or a pen, circle the thing you want gone. The circled part is tinted so you can see what will be removed.

### Add to or subtract from the area

The **Draw area** buttons at the bottom of the editor control the circled part. Remove Objects uses them the same way other tools that work on part of a photo do.

- **Draw area** turns drawing on and off. You don't need to turn it off to move around: use two fingers to pan and zoom the photo while it is on. Zoom in for small objects.
- **+** adds to the area. Circle more to remove as well. Use it to catch a part you missed, or to pick a second object.
- **−** takes part of the area back out. Circle the part you want to keep.
- **Clear** removes the whole area so you can start over.

Circling when none of the buttons is on starts a new area and replaces the old one, so tap **+** to keep what you have.

### Remove two things at once

The area doesn't have to be one shape. Circle the first object, tap **+**, then circle the second one somewhere else in the photo. Both stay tinted, and one **Remove** takes out both.

### Pick fast or best quality, then tap Remove

Choose **Object removal (fast)** or **Object removal (best quality)**, then tap **Remove**. A progress notice shows while it works.

### Check the result

The area is filled in and counts as one step in your project. Undo it if you don't like it.

**Tips:** circle a little past the edge of the object so no outline is left behind, and remove big things in a few smaller passes.
