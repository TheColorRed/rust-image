# Beauty

**ID:** `alakazam.beauty`

Adds the **Beauty** tab controls for portraits. Edits apply to one person or to everyone in the photo.

## Skin

- **Even Skin Tone** evens out blotchy color.
- **Tan** adds a sun-kissed tone.
- **Lighten and Darken** changes skin brightness.
- **Smooth** softens skin texture.

## Retouch

- **Teeth Whitening** brightens teeth.
- **Red Eye** fixes red eyes from flash.
- **Blemish** taps away a spot on the skin.
- **Hair** changes hair color.

## Makeup

Lipstick, Lip Liner, Eyeliner, Eyeshadow, Brow Highlight, Concealer, Blush, Highlighter and Face Shaping.

## Needs

Beauty uses these AI models, which Alakazam downloads when you first need them: person detection, face parsing, face landmarks and skin segmentation. See [Smart features](/guide/smart-features) and [Portraits and faces](/guide/portraits).

## How to use it

### Open the Beauty tab

Open a photo with a face in it and choose the **Beauty** tab. If Beauty isn't there, install it from **Plugins** first.

### Pick a control

Pick a control, such as **Lipstick**. The first time, Alakazam may ask to download the face models it needs.

### Choose who it applies to

If the photo has more than one person, choose who the edit applies to: one person or everyone.

### Choose a color and strength

Choose a color. Lipstick, hair, eyeshadow and the other makeup controls offer named swatches such as "Classic red" or "Auburn". Then use the sliders to set how strong it is. The photo updates as you drag.

### Fix small spots

To fix something small, like **Blemish** or **Red Eye**, zoom in first and tap the spot.

### Change it later

Each control is its own step in the project, so you can open it again later to change or remove it.

**Tip:** do skin first (Even Skin Tone, Smooth), then teeth and hair, then makeup on top.
