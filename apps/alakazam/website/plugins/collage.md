# Collage

**ID:** `alakazam.collage`

Adds a **Collage** workspace that creates a new image from a selection of photos. Choose your photos, pick a layout such as a grid or overlapping layers, and Alakazam builds the finished image.

Collage has no AI model dependencies.

## How to use it

### Open Collage

Open **Collage** from the home screen. If it isn't there, install it from **Plugins**.

### Choose images and size

Under **Image**, set the **Image Size** and choose **Collage Images**. You can pick from your photos or files, and pick many at once.

### Choose a style

Under **Layout**, choose a **Style**:

- **Grid**: even cells in columns and rows. Photos are cropped to fit.
- **Layered**: overlapping cells you can rotate and scale.
- **Random**: photos scattered around the canvas.
- **Auto**: arranges the photos by their shapes, so less is cropped.

Each style adds its own controls, such as columns and rows. **Trim Edges** cuts away empty bands when a style leaves them.

### Rotate and scale

Under **Options**, use **Rotation** and **Scale** if the style allows it.

### Add a frame and shadow

Under **Effects**, add a **Frame** (None, Border or Polaroid) and a **Shadow**.

### Add text

Under **Text**, pick a **Font**, write **Words**, then set the size, color and position (Top, Middle or Bottom). Download a font first if the list is empty.

### Edit one photo

Tap a photo in the preview to change just that cell: swap its image, move or zoom the picture inside it, or, with Polaroid frames, write a caption on its strip. **Apply words to** can send a caption to the current photo, matching ones, or all.
