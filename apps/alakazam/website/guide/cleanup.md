# Clean up a photo

## Cleanup

The **Cleanup** section has two controls for photos with flaws:

- **Smooth Noise** reduces the grainy speckles you often see in dark or low-light photos.
- **Remove Dust** cleans up small specks.

## Red Eye and Blemish

These two are in the **Beauty** section.

- **Red Eye:** flash photos can make eyes look red. Open **Red Eye** and fix each eye.
- **Blemish:** tap a spot on the skin to smooth it away. Zoom in first so you can tap accurately.

## Tips

- Zoom in before you tap, so you hit the right spot.
- Do one thing at a time. Every edit is a separate step, so you can undo one without losing the rest.
