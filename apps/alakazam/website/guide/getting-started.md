# Getting started

Alakazam gives you photo editing tools that are easy to use. Choose an edit, adjust it with simple controls, and see the result as you work.

## Open a photo

Open Alakazam, pick a photo from your phone, and it opens in the editor.

- **Zoom in** by pinching, and **move around** by dragging, to check the small details.
- Changes show on the photo **as you move a slider**, so you can stop when it looks right.

## Around the app

Three tabs along the bottom of the home screen take you around the app. They're hidden while you edit a photo, because the editor takes over the whole screen. Close the editor to see them again.

- **Home:** your projects, and the way to add photos. See [Projects](/guide/projects).
- **Settings:** light or dark, and the file you get when you share. See [Settings](/guide/settings).
- **Plugins:** AI models and add-ons that can add editing tools. See [Plugins](/guide/plugins).

## Find an edit

Edits are grouped into sections. They appear in this order:

| Section | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Mood** | Whole-photo looks: Vibrant, Warm, Golden Hour, Vintage, Noir, Moody, Sunny, Cold, Fade, Sepia, Dreamy, Cloudy, Midnight, Ocean, Forest, Frost and Neon. |
| **Adjustments** | Brightness, Contrast, Bright & Dark Areas, Colorfulness, Light and Boost Color. |
| **Beauty** | Skin, Teeth Whitening, Red Eye, Blemish, Makeup and Hair. |
| **Transform** | Rotate Left, Rotate Right, Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical and Crop. |
| **Sharpen** | Sharpen. |
| **Effects** | Black & White, Flip Colors, Auto Fix, Auto Colors, Black & White Only, Vignette and Flat Colors. |
| **Special Effects** | Color Splash, Infrared, Chiaroscuro, Cyanotype, Faded Film, Bleach Bypass, Split Tone, Glitch Art, Bokeh, Night Vision, Thermal, Pop Art and Vaporwave. |
| **Blur** | Blur and Blur (Sharp Edges). |
| **Detail** | Soften. |
| **Cleanup** | Smooth Noise and Remove Dust. |
