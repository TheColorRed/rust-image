# Give a photo a look

A look changes the feel of the whole photo. Pick one, then drag its slider until it feels right. Every look is shown as a preview of your photo, so you can see the result before you pick it.

## Mood

**Mood** looks are gentle and suit most photos.

| Look | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Vibrant** | Makes colors stronger, especially the dull ones. |
| **Warm** | Adds a warm orange tint, like indoor light. |
| **Golden Hour** | A warmer tint plus a little extra brightness, like the glow of late-day sun. |
| **Vintage** | A faint brown tint with softer contrast and less color, like an old print. |
| **Noir** | Removes all color and adds contrast, for a classic black-and-white film look. |
| **Moody** | A cool tint with more contrast and a darker photo. |
| **Sunny** | A light warm tint, brighter, with livelier color. |
| **Cold** | Adds a cool blue tint, like a winter day. |
| **Fade** | Softer contrast and less color, for a washed-out look. |
| **Sepia** | A brown, old-photograph tint. |
| **Dreamy** | A faint warm tint with soft contrast and gentle color. |
| **Cloudy** | A cool, slightly gray tint with muted color and a touch less brightness. |
| **Midnight** | A deep blue, darker photo with more contrast. |
| **Ocean** | An underwater blue-green tint. |
| **Forest** | A deep green tint. |
| **Frost** | A cold, dark blue tint. |
| **Neon** | A magenta tint. |

## Effects

**Effects** are simple changes to the whole photo.

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Black & White** | Turns the photo gray, keeping all its light and dark. |
| **Black & White Only** | Makes every part either pure black or pure white. The slider sets how much turns black. |
| **Flip Colors** | Swaps every color for its opposite, like a photo negative. |
| **Vignette** | Darkens the corners so the middle of the photo stands out. |
| **Flat Colors** | Cuts the photo down to a few colors. The slider sets how many, and fewer looks more like a poster. |
| **Auto Fix** and **Auto Colors** | One-tap repairs. See [Fix and adjust a photo](/guide/adjust). |

## Special Effects

**Special Effects** are bold looks for when you want a photo to stand out.

| Effect | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Color Splash** | Keeps the colors you choose and turns everything else black and white. |
| **Infrared** | A dreamlike false-color look. Leaves and grass go pink or pale, and skies and water go deep blue. |
| **Chiaroscuro** | Dramatic light and shadow, like a single light falling on the subject. The shadows go much darker and the lights stay bright. |
| **Cyanotype** | An old blueprint print in deep blue and white. |
| **Faded Film** | A soft, matte film look with lifted blacks and gentle highlights. You can make it warmer or cooler. |
| **Bleach Bypass** | A harsh, gritty film look with strong contrast and muted color. |
| **Split Tone** | A warm brown print with cool blue in only the deepest shadows. |
| **Glitch Art** | Slides the colors apart so edges get a neon fringe, like a broken digital screen. |
| **Bokeh** | Adds glowing, blurred circles of light, like out-of-focus lights in the background. |
| **Night Vision** | A green night-camera view, with grain, scan lines and dark edges. |
| **Thermal** | A heat-camera view. Dark areas go blue, then cyan and yellow, and bright areas go red. |
| **Pop Art** | A comic-book print: flat blocks of bright color with outlines and dots. |
| **Vaporwave** | An 80s neon look in purple, pink and cyan, with a glowing sun, a grid floor and video grain. |

## Tips

- If a look isn't right, remove that step. The rest of your edits stay.
